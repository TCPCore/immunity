# Halcyon Robotics, Pricing (2026)

Halcyon Robotics sells fleet software for warehouse robotics. Two plans are
offered on annual terms.

| Plan | Price | Included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enterprise | 499 USD per seat per year | Dedicated support, SSO, audit log |
| Team | 99 USD per seat per year | Shared support, no SSO |

Discounts above 20% require Finance approval. Prices are quoted in USD and are
not inclusive of tax.
